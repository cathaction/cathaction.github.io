Overview

Leaderboard Governance. The CATHACTION organisers maintain this leaderboard across all CATHACTION tracks and challenge editions. Test labels are never released, and every score on the leaderboard is computed by the organisers.
  • Tracks: CATHACTION-Seg (catheter and guidewire segmentation); CATHACTION-Collision and CATHACTION-Action to follow.
  • Editions: MICCAI 2026 Challenge, CATHACTION-Seg.
  • Entries from every edition and from open submissions share one leaderboard; the source of each entry is shown.

An entry is listed only after the following requirements are met:

  1. The submitted Docker image is run by the organisers on the human test set and the animal and phantom test set.
  2. All training data is declared, including external and private data.
  3. The training data audit is passed.

What to Submit for Evaluation

  • A Docker image that runs offline on one GPU.
  • Input /input/image.nii.gz, a stack of 2D X-ray frames; output /output/segmentation.nii.gz of the same size.
  • Labels: 0 background, 1 catheter, 2 guidewire.
  • Team name and a short method description.
  • A complete list of training data.

Leaderboard Publication Details

Official Container Evaluation: CATHACTION-Seg

  • Human test set: 11,723 frames, 241 sequences, 4 hospitals (CLY, DHW, HTM, HXS).
  • Animal and phantom test set: 4,691 frames (animal 1,006, phantom 3,685).
  • Ranking metric: case-macro Dice. Also reported: clDice, precision, recall, HD95 and empty prediction rate.
  • The same scoring code is used for every submission.

Training Data Audit

  • The declared training data is checked against the submitted image.
  • Performance on public training data and on the test set is compared to detect use of test labels.
  • The training data category is shown on the leaderboard: human, animal and phantom only, or not declared.

Eligibility and Review

  • Undeclared training data or use of test labels leads to removal from the leaderboard.
  • Each entry shows its evaluation date; new entries are announced in News.
  • Teams may ask for their entry to be updated or withdrawn.

Protocol Updates

The organisers may revise this protocol. Changes are dated on this page and announced in News.
